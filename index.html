Welcome to COLD WAR

Welcome to the COLD WAR website, a comprehensive resource dedicated to exploring the historical period of the Cold War. This website provides valuable information, insights, and resources related to the Cold War era, including key events, influential figures, and significant speeches that shaped the geopolitical landscape of the time.

What is COLD WAR

COLD WAR is a project for the study of the Cold War, a period of geopolitical tension between the United States and the Soviet Union that lasted from the end of World War II in 1945 until the early 1990s. The project aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of the political, social, and cultural aspects of this era, as well as its impact on international relations and global history.
