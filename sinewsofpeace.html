The Sinews of Peace

In 1946, Winston Churchill delivered a speech at Westminster College in Fulton, Missouri, which became known as "The Sinews of Peace." In this speech, Churchill emphasized the importance of unity and cooperation among nations to prevent future conflicts and promote peace. He highlighted the need for strong alliances and the role of democracy in maintaining global stability. The speech is remembered for its call to action and its vision for a world where nations work together to ensure lasting peace.

In this speech, Churchill warned about the expansionist policies of the Soviet Union and the threat of communism. He called for a strong alliance between Western nations to counter this threat and promote democratic values. The speech is often remembered for its famous line, "From Stettin in the Baltic to Trieste in the Adriatic, an iron curtain has descended across the Continent."
